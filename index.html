Selected work

Two products, shipped solo and running today.

StillFiring

stillfiring.com

Shopify merchants lose ad budget for weeks before anyone notices a pixel stopped firing. StillFiring watches the storefront and tells them the hour it breaks.

What it does
Rescans the public storefront hourly, diffs the tracking surface, and emails the merchant when a pixel disappears, duplicates, or stops sandboxing.
Hardest part
Naming every pixel from a live storefront with no app install and no admin session, then reconciling recorded orders against Google Ads conversion counts.
Business model
Free one-off scan as the front door. Monitoring at $29 per store per month with a 14 day trial, billed through Shopify.
  • Shopify App
  • Custom Pixels
  • Google Ads API
  • GA4
  • Meta Pixel
  • Hourly monitoring

ProvePixel

provepixel.com

Agencies pitch prospects with generic advice and get ignored. ProvePixel scans a list of domains and hands back a specific, checkable defect to open the conversation with.

What it does
Reads tag containers rather than page source, then flags dead properties, duplicate pixels, certificate failures, missing Consent Mode, and abandoned tag managers.
Hardest part
Grading every finding as Confirmed, Likely, or Worth checking. An agency that sends a wrong claim to a prospect never sends a second one.
Business model
$299 per month for 500 scans, $499 for 2,500 scans across multiple brands. Output is a one page report under the sender's own branding.
  • Domain scanning
  • GTM container parsing
  • Consent Mode v2
  • White label reports
  • Bulk domain queues
